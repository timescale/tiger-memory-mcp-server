Tiger Memory

In quiet bins where whispers go to rest,
the server keeps a ledger of the best,
small sparks of thought, a trail of minted light,
recalled on cue, returned in steady night.

APIs hum, a thoughtful, gentle thread,
remembering the things the models said,
each migration lays a careful, patient seam,
so time can fold and keep the meaning clean.

Here, tools converse in standards, crisp and clear,
the MCP keeps the story close and near,
and through the keys, the notes we choose to save
rise up again, a tiger's memory brave.
